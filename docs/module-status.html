Combat Automation Core

  • Working As Intended: Target number calculation with aim, range, rate of fire, and the ±60 modifier clamp.
  • Working As Intended: Multi-hit resolution including Storm, Twin-linked adjustments, and evasion reducing hits.
  • Working As Intended: Hit location resolution (reverse digits) and chained additional locations.
  • Needs Tweaking: Presentation clarity in chat cards when multiple modifiers stack from traits, psychic strength, and manual edits.

Damage & Weapon Trait Handling

  • Working As Intended: Trait mutation pipeline for Tearing, Proven, Primitive, Accurate, and related damage adjustments.
  • Needs Tweaking: Visibility of intermediate damage formula mutations for GMs diagnosing unexpected totals.
  • Planned Feature: Expanded trait coverage for more niche qualities and situational modifiers.

NPC Workflow & Minion Support
