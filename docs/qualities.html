Item Qualities & Modifiers

Weapon qualities are the fastest way to change outcomes. This page documents the traits that currently mutate formulas or hit counts.

← Back to guide index

Damage-altering Qualities

Tearing

  • Adds one extra die to the damage roll.
  • The lowest die is dropped (implemented via dl).
  • Only applied if the formula does not already include drop/keep syntax.

Proven (X)

  • Adds minX to the damage dice expression.
  • Dice results below X are raised to X by the dice engine.

Primitive (X)

  • Adds maxX to the damage dice expression.
  • Dice results above X are lowered to X by the dice engine.

Accurate

  • Requires aiming to activate.
  • Adds extra damage dice based on DoS beyond the first.
  • Extra dice: floor((DoS - 1) / 2), capped at 2 additional dice.

Hit-count & Targeting Qualities

Storm

  • Doubles the number of hits after the base multi-hit calculation.

Twin-linked

  • Applies a +20 bonus to hit.
  • When you score enough DoS, it increases hit potential and shots fired.

Inaccurate

  • Forces aim bonuses to 0 even when aiming is selected.

Psychic & Miscellaneous Modifiers

  • Psychic modifiers are driven by (rating - value) × 10.
  • Attack type modifiers stack alongside manual modifiers, aim, range, and trait bonuses.
  • Total modifiers remain subject to the global ±60 clamp.
