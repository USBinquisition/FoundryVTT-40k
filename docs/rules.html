Rules & Mechanics

This page summarises the most important mechanics as they are currently implemented by the automation layer.

Target Numbers & Modifier Caps

  • Total roll modifiers are clamped to +60 / -60 before being applied.
  • The effective target is baseTarget + clamp(totalMods, -60, +60).
  • Combat modifiers combine aim, range, attack type, psychic rating deltas, and manual edits.

See the deeper notes in RULES_REFERENCE.md for the precise breakdown.

Attack Types & Rate of Fire

Attack types drive three important values: the to-hit modifier, the degrees-of-success margin required for extra hits, and the maximum number of hits.

  • Standard: +10 to hit, one hit maximum.
  • Semi-auto / Swift / Barrage: 0 modifier, extra hits every 2 DoS, capped by burst RoF.
  • Full-auto / Lightning: -10 modifier, extra hits every 1 DoS, capped by full RoF.
  • Called shot: -20 modifier, one hit maximum.
  • Charge / All-out: +20 / +30, one hit maximum.
  • Bolt / Blast: handled as single-hit attacks with custom downstream effects.

Degrees of Success & Failure

  • A test succeeds when d100 ≤ target.final.
  • On success: DoS = 1 + (floor(target/10) - floor(result/10)).
  • On failure: DoF = 1 + (floor(result/10) - floor(target/10)).

Multi-hit Resolution

  • Base hits scale with DoS and the attack type margin.
  • Storm doubles the hit count after the base calculation.
  • Twin-linked can add hit potential and shots fired when you score enough DoS.
  • Evasion DoS subtract directly from hits.
  • Hits never drop below zero and are capped by RoF and remaining shots.

Hit Location Behaviour

  • The first hit location is based on the reversed roll (e.g. 05 → 50).
  • Additional hits follow a fixed lookup table that repeats the last entry when exhausted.

Damage Construction Overview

  1. Start from the weapon's damage formula.
  2. Apply trait mutations (such as Tearing, Proven, Primitive, Accurate).
  3. Append flat bonuses (for example, + SB or numeric modifiers).
  4. Resolve actor bonuses and PR substitutions.

The dedicated Item Qualities & Modifiers page breaks down how those traits are currently handled.
