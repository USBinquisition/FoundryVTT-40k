Combat Rules Snapshot

These highlights mirror the currently implemented logic in script/common/roll.js and related files. Use the rules reference for the complete detail.

Target number assembly

  • Combat modifiers are capped to ±60 before applying to the base target.
  • Attack type, aim, range, twin-linked, and psychic modifiers all stack into the final target.

Multi-hit behavior

  • Semi-auto uses a hit margin of 2; full-auto uses a hit margin of 1.
  • Storm doubles hits; twin-linked adds accuracy and can add extra hits at 2+ DoS.
  • Evasion degrees of success reduce total hits directly.

Damage traits

  • Tearing: adds a die and drops the lowest.
  • Proven / Primitive: enforced through dice modifiers.
  • Accurate: adds up to 2d10 extra damage when aiming with sufficient DoS.
  • Razor Sharp: can double penetration at 3+ DoS.
